# FAQs

**1. What if I accidentally clicked a phishing link?**
Report it immediately to IT Security. Do not try to fix it yourself. Disconnect from the network if instructed. You will not be punished for reporting in good faith.

**2. What if I am not sure whether it is a real incident?**
Report it anyway. The security team would rather investigate a false alarm than miss a real incident.

**3. Will I get in trouble for reporting?**
No. DIA has a non-retaliation policy for good-faith reporting. Reporting protects patients and the company.

**4. What if the incident involves my manager or a coworker?**
Report it through the same channels. If you are uncomfortable, use the Compliance Hotline, which allows anonymous reporting.

**5. What if I lose my company laptop while traveling?**
Report it immediately by phone. Do not wait until you return to the office. The security team can remotely wipe the device if needed.

**6. What if I receive a suspicious email but did not click anything?**
Report it to IT Security at security@dia.com. Do not forward it to coworkers.

**7. What if I see someone in a secure area without a badge?**
Do not confront them. Notify Facilities and IT Security immediately. Note their description and location.

**8. What happens after I report?**
The IT Security team will investigate, contain the incident, and notify you if any action is needed. You may be asked to provide additional information.

**9. Will my device be taken away?**
Possibly, if it is needed for forensic investigation. You will be provided a temporary device if needed.

**10. What if I am a contractor or vendor?**
Follow the same reporting process. Notify your company's point of contact and DIA IT Security.
