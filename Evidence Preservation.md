# Evidence Preservation

Preserving evidence helps the security team understand what happened and prevent it from happening again.

## If the Security Incident Involves Email:

- Do not delete the email.
- Do not forward it to anyone except IT Security.
- Do not reply to the sender.
- Take a screenshot if safe to do so.

## If the Secruity Incident Involves a Device:

- Disconnect from the network (unplug Ethernet, turn off Wi-Fi).
- Do not power off.
- Do not plug in USB drives or external devices.
- Leave the device where it is.

## If the Security Incident Involves Paper Records:

- Do not shred, alter, or move the records.
- Secure the area if possible.
- Notify IT Security and Compliance immediately.

## If the incident Involves a Verbal Conversation:

- Write down what was said as soon as possible.
- Include date, time, names, and location.
- Do not discuss with others.

## Evidence Log:

Use the [Evidence Log Template](templates/evidence-log-template.md) to document what you observed and preserved.
