# What Not to Do 


| Do NOT | Why |
|---|---|
| Power off the device | Destroys forensic evidence |
| Delete the suspicious email or file | Removes evidence the team needs |
| Run your own antivirus scan | Can alter or delete evidence |
| Click more links or open more attachments | May spread malware |
| Forward the suspicious email to coworkers | Spreads the threat |
| Post about the incident on social media | Can create legal and reputational risk |
| Try to fix it yourself | May make the incident worse |
| Wait to report | Every minute counts |

**Warning!** "The Most Common Mistake"
    Employees often try to fix the problem themselves before reporting. This destroys evidence and delays response. **Report first. Fix later.**
