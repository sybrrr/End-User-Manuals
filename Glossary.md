# Glossary

**Breach:** Unauthorized access to or disclosure of protected health information (PHI).

**Confidentiality:** Ensuring information is only accessible to authorized individuals.

**Containment:** The process of stopping an incident from spreading.

**Evidence:** Any information (files, logs, screenshots, devices) that helps the security team understand what happened.

**Forensics:** The process of collecting and analyzing evidence after an incident.

**HIPAA:** Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act — federal law protecting PHI.

**Incident:** Any event that threatens the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of data, systems, or facilities.

**Integrity:** Ensuring information is accurate and has not been altered.

**Malware:** Malicious software designed to damage or gain unauthorized access to systems.

**Protected Health Information (PHI):** Any information about a patient's health, treatment, or payment that can identify them.

**Phishing:** A fraudulent attempt to obtain sensitive information by disguising as a trustworthy entity.

**Ransomware:** Malware that locks files or systems and demands payment to unlock them.

**Remediation:** The process of fixing the root cause of an incident.

**SOC 2:** A compliance framework for service organizations that handle customer data.

**Social Engineering:** Manipulating people to reveal confidential information.

**Tailgating:** Following an authorized person into a secure area without using your own badge.
